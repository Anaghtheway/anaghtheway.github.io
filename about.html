01 / Research

Small channels.
Interesting questions.

What happens when a coolant can absorb heat through a phase change, but the flow doesn't mix enough to make full use of it?

That question is at the heart of the research I coauthored on herringbone microchannels and microencapsulated phase-change material slurries. The study numerically explores how passive mixing can improve heat transfer, while accounting for the pressure-drop penalty.
